## Brand & Style

The design system powers an elite, cyberpunk-inspired dark sports command center built specifically for European football fans managing nocturnal schedules. It evokes the feeling of a focused Bloomberg terminal fused with an eSports mission control: quantitative, sharp, mission-critical, and unapologetic.

The design movement is **Technical Cyberpunk Minimalism**: ultra-dense data cards, obsidian depths, 1px micro-borders, laser-sharp monospaced telemetry, and neon status accents. It avoids decorative clutter or bloated card margins, maintaining a disciplined 100vh zero-outer-scroll container where every pixel serves tactical clarity and circadian decision-making.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in pitch-black and cosmic obsidian tones, layered with surgical luminous highlights:

- **Primary Amber Gold (`#FFB800`)**: Reserved for high-value hierarchy anchors—Match of the Night (Hero), highest algorithm ROI badges, 3-star ratings, active primary tabs, and critical CTAs.
- **Secondary Cyan/Teal (`#44E2CD`)**: Represents low sleep sacrifice, safe biometric budget ranges, positive DP solver margins, and active spoiler-protection mode.
- **Tertiary Mystic Purple (`#CEB5FF`)**: Signifies narrative weight—derby clash tags, historical grudge storylines, and rivalry headers.
- **Live Neon Coral (`#E24B4A`)**: A strictly reserved status color for running live matches, real-time clock pulses, live scores, and critical sleep minefield warnings.
- **Neutral Surface Hierarchy**: From darkest outer canvas (`#0A0D14`) to intermediate sidebars (`#0E121B`), active cards (`#131722`), and active hover/selection states (`#1A2133`). All structural separation relies on 1px precision hairline borders in `#232A3B`.

## Typography

The type ecosystem uses a tri-font hierarchy:

1. **Titles and Structural Landmarks (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Geometric, authoritative, and contemporary. Delivers clean, architectural headers that resist decorative flourish.
2. **Body & Descriptive Storylines (`Be Vietnam Pro`)**: Highly legible at small scales (11px to 13px), open apertures, robust vertical metrics that excel in tight multi-line cards under low light.
3. **Quantitative Readouts & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict tabular layout for live scores, wall-clock time, countdown timers, sleep costs (S0–S4), and algorithm ROI metrics. Numbers never jitter during real-time status updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The workstation enforces a desktop-first, fixed 100vh dual-column split layout (`overflow: hidden` on viewport):

- **macOS Window Integration**: The 52px top bar reserves `pl-20` (80px) on the top-left to clear standard macOS window traffic lights seamlessly.
- **Split Workspace Distribution**:
  - **Left Tactical Column (38% split, min 420px, max 520px)**: Houses navigation switcher tabs, hero match card, and scrollable virtual lists with customized narrow scrollbars.
  - **Right Command Stage (62% split, remaining flex)**: Houses either the high-definition live stream player, deep tactical intelligence cards, or visual sleep matrices.
- **Micro-Density Spacing**: Uses an ultra-compact 4px/8px modular base scale (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 12px) to maximize data density and eliminate wasted negative space while maintaining readability.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is produced through layered tonal tiers and crisp perimeter boundaries rather than blurry natural shadows:

- **Level 0 (App Canvas)**: Pure deep midnight obsidian (`#0A0D14`).
- **Level 1 (Dock & Sidebars)**: Structural paneling (`#0E121B`) enclosed by a 1px solid border (`#232A3B`).
- **Level 2 (Containers & Cards)**: Dark slate surfaces (`#131722`) framed with hairline outlines (`#232A3B`).
- **Level 3 (Focused Match Highlight / Hero)**: Dark gradient underlay with an illuminated hairline amber border glow (`border-color: rgba(255, 184, 0, 0.40)`).
- **Overlay & Sliding Drawers**: Semitransparent slate backdrop (`rgba(10, 13, 20, 0.75)`) reinforced by heavy hardware-accelerated blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces a structured, industrial feel using Soft roundedness (`0.25rem` / `4px` base):

- **Data Cards and Containers**: `rounded-md` (6px) or `rounded-lg` (8px) for clean, tech-inspired corners.
- **Badges and Filters**: Controlled `rounded-sm` (3px) to `rounded-md` (6px) for telemetry indicators, retaining rectangular discipline.
- **Club Badges**: Strict geometric circles (`rounded-full`, 28px to 36px) featuring high-contrast 3-letter capital club acronyms (ARS, MCI, INT, MIL) on solid club hues, eliminating third-party raster image assets.
- **Interactive Toggles & Pills**: Subtle radiuses that nest naturally within input bounds.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Hero CTA**: Solid Amber Gold (`#FFB800`), bold black text (`#0A0D14`), uppercase telemetry font (`JetBrains Mono`), flat with zero blur, hover state brightens to `#FFC426`.
- **Secondary Action**: Translucent dark slate (`#131722`) with 1px `#232A3B` border, text in `#F1F5F9`, hover shifts to `#1A2133`.
- **Destructive/Minefield Warning**: Outlined red-coral (`#E24B4A`/30) over tinted backdrop (`rgba(226, 75, 74, 0.08)`).

### Club Badges
- 32px or 28px circular SVG/div tokens with 2px inner border, hosting bold 3-letter abbreviations in `JetBrains Mono` bold (ARS in deep crimson, MCI in sky-blue, INT in royal blue/black, MIL in red/charcoal).

### Sleep Tier & ROI Chips
- Compact badges (`h-5` or `h-6`) composed of a 1px solid perimeter and `bg-opacity-10` tint:
  - **S0 (Zero Cost)**: Cyan border and text (`#44E2CD`).
  - **S1–S2 (Mild/Moderate)**: Slate/Teal subtle outline.
  - **S3–S4 (Severe Loss)**: Coral/Red alert border (`#E24B4A`).
  - **ROI Badges**: Amber gold border and numerals (`#FFB800`).

### Match Cards
- 1px hairline border `#232A3B`, base background `#131722`.
- Active live match adds a solid 3px left indicator line in `#FFB800` or `#E24B4A`, accompanied by a pulsing coral beacon on live minutes.
- Anti-spoiler mode blurs the score container using `filter: blur(8px)` with an eye reveal action pill.

### Sliders & Slotted Inputs
- 4px height track in `#232A3B` filled with gradient `#44E2CD` to `#FFB800`.
- Circular amber thumb (14px) with high-contrast inner pip and numerical value mirrored directly in adjacent JetBrains Mono display readouts.
